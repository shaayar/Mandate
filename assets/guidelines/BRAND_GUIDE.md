# Mandate Brand Quick Guide

**Identity:** Mandate  
**Concept:** The Authority Gate  
**Descriptor:** Policy enforcement for AI agents.

## Voice
Precise, calm, technical, trustworthy. Avoid claims that imply absolute security or guarantee agent behavior.

## Colors
Midnight Navy `#0B1F26`; Deep Teal `#0E8F80`; Electric Teal `#14DBA6`; Off White `#F8FAFC`; Ink `#10252B`; Muted Slate `#5F747B`.

## Typography
Use Space Grotesk for headings and Inter for body text where available. System sans-serif fallbacks are acceptable.

## Logo
Use horizontal logo for repository headers, stacked logo for square layouts, and the icon-only mark for compact surfaces. Keep clear space and preserve the proportions. Avoid effects and low-contrast placements.
